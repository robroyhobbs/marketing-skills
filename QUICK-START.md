# Quick Start Guide

**Super simple: What to type when you need something**

---

## 🆘 "I Don't Know What I Need"

```

```

Tell it what you're trying to achieve. It'll recommend what to do.

## 🎯 The Right Order

Not sure what to do first? Follow this:

1. **Don't have brand voice?** → `/brand-voice`
2. **Don't know what makes you different?** → `/positioning-angles`
3. **Don't know what to write about?** → `/keyword-research`
4. **Don't have a way to get emails?** → `/lead-magnet`
5. **Don't have a landing page?** → `/direct-response-copy`
6. **Don't have email sequences?** → `/email-sequences`
7. **Don't have content?** → `/seo-content` or `/newsletter`
8. **Don't have social content?** → `/content-atomizer`

**Or just run `/marketing-suite:audit` and it tells you.**

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

**Starting a new project?**
- Run `/orchestrator` first
- Let it create your roadmap
- Follow its recommendations

**Need it done fast?**
- Run `/marketing-suite:launch`
- Choose "emergency mode"
- Get bare minimum to launch

**Copy not converting?**
- Launch `copy-editor` agent
- Paste your current copy
- Get specific improvements

**Stuck at a revenue plateau?**
- Launch `marketing-strategist` agent
- Share your metrics
- Get a growth plan
