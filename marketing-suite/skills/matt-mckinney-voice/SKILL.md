## Writing Style

### Tone
- **Direct and real** - Say what you mean, no hedging
- **Problem-solver's voice** - Here's the issue, here's how to fix it
- **Grounded in experience** - Practitioner, not theorist
- **Anti-fluff** - If it sounds like marketing copy, cut it
- **Concise** - Short sentences. Get to the point.

### Structure Patterns
1. **Problem -> Solution -> Impact** - The core arc
2. **Open with quantified pain** - "5+ hours weekly" "30% more support tickets"
3. **One strong conceptual anchor** - Create a memorable framework
4. **Before/After comparison** - Show the transformation clearly
5. **"What you get" clarity** - Concrete deliverables, not vague benefits
6. **Metrics that matter** - ROI framing, time saved

### Conceptual Frameworks (Signature Moves)
- **"Knowledge Fabric"** - Documentation as connective tissue
- **"Drift = Silent Tax"** - Hidden costs made visceral
- **"Living Documentation"** - Docs that evolve with code
- Create NEW frameworks for new topics

### NEVER Do
- Generic AI openings ("In today's fast-paced world...")
- Marketing fluff ("revolutionary," "game-changing," "transform your...")
- Verbose sentences - if you can say it in 10 words, don't use 20
- Features without the "so what"
- Vague claims without specifics
- Padding paragraphs - every sentence earns its place

## The Interview Workflow

Before writing, ask these questions:

**1. The Hook**
> What prompted this article? (news, customer question, product release)

**2. The Audience**
> Who is the target reader? What do they care about?

**3. The Core Insight**
> What's the one thing readers should understand after reading?

**4. The Customer Angle**
> What pain point does this address? What's the business value?

**5. The Example**
> What concrete example or use case illustrates the point?

**6. The Call to Action**
> What should readers do after reading?

## Article Templates

### Problem/Solution Article

```markdown
# [Headline that states the problem or solution]

[1-2 sentences: The problem, quantified if possible]

## What's Actually Happening
[Explain the issue concretely]

## The Fix
[Solution with clear structure]

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| [Pain] | [Relief] |

## What You Get
[Concrete deliverables or outcomes]

## Next Step
[Single clear action]
```

### Comparison Piece

```markdown
# [Headline framing the choice]

[Why this matters now - 1-2 sentences]

| Factor | Option A | Option B |
|--------|----------|----------|
| [X] | [Detail] | [Detail] |
| [Y] | [Detail] | [Detail] |

## The Bottom Line
[Direct recommendation based on use case]
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Opens with real problem (not generic)
